How it’s made

Four steps.
No shortcuts in any of them.

Namkeen is simple food, which is exactly why it is unforgiving. Everything depends on the flour, the oil temperature and how quickly the masala meets the sev after it leaves the kadhai.

01 — Grind

Masala, weekly

Chilli, coriander, dry mango and clove are roasted and ground in-house in small lots. Pre-ground masala loses its top note within days; ours never sits that long.

02 — Press

Besan through the sancha

Gram flour is kneaded to a specific slackness — judged by hand — then pressed through a plate sized for each recipe. Ratlami is coarse; Ujjaini is a hair’s width.

03 — Fry

Clean oil, watched

Batches are small so the oil temperature never crashes. It is filtered constantly and changed after every second batch, which is what keeps the finish clean rather than heavy.

04 — Toss & seal

While still warm

Masala goes on within minutes, while the sev is warm enough to take it. Packs are nitrogen-flushed and sealed the same day, with the batch and date printed on the back.

Questions

Good to know

Where do I buy it?

Every pack is listed on Amazon, and the buttons on this page take you straight to the listing — so delivery, payment, tracking and returns are all handled by Amazon. In Gwalior you can also walk into the shop at Phalka Bazar, Lashkar. For bulk or distributor orders, call us directly on +91 94253 07800.

How long does a pack stay fresh?

Best within six months of the manufacture date printed on the back. Packs are nitrogen-flushed and foil-laminated, so an unopened pack holds its crunch for that whole window. Once opened, seal it tight and finish it within about two weeks — moisture is the only real enemy.

What exactly is in it?

Gram flour, edible vegetable oil, rice flakes, peanuts, chickpeas, lentils and our own spice blend — red chilli, clove, cumin, dry mango, asafoetida, black pepper, turmeric and salt. Full ingredient and nutritional panels are printed on every pack. Contains peanuts and soy; made in a facility that also handles nuts, seeds and gluten.

How spicy is spicy?

Every product card carries a four-bar heat scale. Sada Sev sits at one bar — genuinely mild, safe for children. Lahsun Mix sits at four and is built for people who already know they like garlic and chilli together. Ratlami Sev at three is the classic Malwa heat: it builds rather than hits.

Yes. We work with retailers, caterers and corporate gifting clients, and several distributor territories are currently open. Call +91 94253 07800 or write to cacamitnamkeen@gmail.com with your city and expected monthly volume, and we will send pricing and terms.

Is everything vegetarian?

Every product carries the green vegetarian mark. No animal-derived ingredient goes into any recipe, and the same is true of the oil we fry in.
